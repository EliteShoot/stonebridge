Sell My House Fast in Ferndale, MI

Need to sell your house fast in Ferndale, Michigan? Start with the facts that affect your sale: the home’s condition, who lives there and when you need to move. StoneBridge reviews Metro Detroit properties for a possible direct purchase. You can ask for a review before you decide how to sell.

Request a private property review →

Describe how the property is used

Tell us whether your Ferndale property is a single home, a rental or a property with more than one living space. Share the current use and any records you have. A finished basement or extra kitchen should not be treated as a separate legal unit without checking.

Compare small repairs with a full update

Paint and cleanup are different from roof, plumbing or foundation work. Split your list into small jobs and larger repairs. Then compare the cost and time of each plan with a direct sale in the home’s current condition.

What to send for a property review

A short, clear summary makes it easier to discuss the next step. Include the details you know. If something is unclear, say so.

  • Full address and your connection to the property.
  • Current condition, recent photos and known repair needs.
  • Occupancy, access and your preferred closing date.

Choose the sale terms that fit

A quick sale is not always the highest-priced sale. Compare the money you would receive after costs, the work required and the risk of delays. Ask for written terms before making a choice. StoneBridge does not require you to accept an offer after an inquiry.
