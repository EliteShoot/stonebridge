PRIVATE ACQUISITIONS

Considering selling
a property?

StoneBridge buys Michigan residential properties directly from owners, including inherited homes, properties facing foreclosure, houses needing repairs, vacant or rental properties, and select vacant land. If you are considering selling, tell us about the property for a private, no-obligation review. Qualified transactions can close in as little as 7 days.

SELL TO STONEBRIDGE

Selling a property
in Michigan?

Not every property sale needs a public listing. StoneBridge reviews homes and residential property directly from Michigan owners, including situations where speed, privacy, repairs, inheritance, or an upcoming foreclosure may make a direct sale worth considering.

01

INHERITED HOMES

Inherited a Michigan house you do not plan to keep? Submit the property for a direct acquisition review, including homes that need updating or repairs.

02

FACING FORECLOSURE

Owners facing foreclosure can submit a property for review. Because lender and legal deadlines matter, contacting us early may provide more time to evaluate a potential sale.

03

HOUSES NEEDING REPAIRS

We consider distressed houses, deferred maintenance, outdated homes, and properties that may require substantial renovation.

04

VACANT & RENTAL HOMES

If you no longer want to maintain a vacant house or rental property, StoneBridge can review it as a potential direct acquisition.

05

VACANT LAND & LOTS

StoneBridge also reviews select Michigan residential lots, vacant land, and acreage for potential acquisition and future development.

06

FAST, PRIVATE CLOSING

Qualified transactions can close in as little as 7 days. The property is negotiated directly with StoneBridge without a public listing through StoneBridge, subject to title and closing requirements.
