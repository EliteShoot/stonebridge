IMPORTANT CONSIDERATIONS

Understand the property
and your timeline.

A direct sale is only one possible path. Foreclosure circumstances vary, and deadlines can matter. StoneBridge does not provide legal, foreclosure, tax, or financial advice.

01

KNOW YOUR DEADLINES

Review notices and communications you have received from your lender, servicer, court, or other parties. Do not assume contacting a buyer changes any deadline.

02

REQUEST A PAYOFF

The mortgage payoff and other amounts affecting title can be important when evaluating whether a sale can be completed and what proceeds may remain at closing.

03

REVIEW TITLE

Liens, taxes, ownership interests, judgments, or other title matters may need to be addressed before or at closing.

04

COMPARE YOUR OPTIONS

Depending on your circumstances, you may want to compare a direct sale with a traditional sale and discuss lender alternatives or other options with qualified professionals.

05

ACT EARLY

More time can provide more room to evaluate a sale and complete title, payoff, and closing requirements. A last-minute submission does not guarantee a transaction can close before a foreclosure event.

06

NO OBLIGATION

Submitting your property only asks StoneBridge to review the opportunity. It does not require you to sell, guarantee an offer, or create an agreement.

StoneBridge Development Group is a prospective property buyer, not your attorney, lender, housing counselor, tax adviser, or foreclosure adviser. Submitting a property does not stop or delay foreclosure. Consider contacting your mortgage servicer and an appropriate attorney or HUD-approved housing counselor for guidance specific to your circumstances.
