Sell My House Fast in Grosse Pointe, MI

Considering a sale in the Grosse Pointe area? Send the full address, the municipality on your property records and a description of the home. We’ll look at its condition and your plans before discussing whether a direct purchase could fit.

Tell us about your property →

Start with the exact city and address

A Grosse Pointe mailing address is only the starting point. Share the city shown on your property records, the parcel number and the full address. This helps a buyer check the right records and compare the right homes. A well-kept house and a house needing major work call for different sale plans.

Make a plan for the home’s contents

For a family home, decide what you want to keep before you plan a cleanout. Note roof, basement and heating issues separately from cosmetic updates. Share any repair reports you already have. Ask the buyer to state which items must leave and which may stay.

What to send for a property review

Send what you know about the home. You don’t need to have every answer before contacting us.

  • Full address and your connection to the property.
  • Current condition, recent photos and known repair needs.
  • Occupancy, access and your preferred closing date.

Choose the sale terms that fit

Look at what you would receive after costs, how much preparation each option needs and what could delay closing. Ask for the terms in writing. Contacting StoneBridge does not commit you to selling.
