Selling a rental or a home you have moved out of

An empty home can still mean maintenance, utilities and trips back to the property. A rental comes with a different set of decisions. Tell us whether anyone lives there, whether a lease is in place and how a visit can be arranged.

You do not need to make the home vacant just to ask about a sale. Explain the current situation so we can discuss access and the next steps with the right information.

Decide whether repairs are worth taking on

Before paying for updates, compare the likely cost and time with selling the house in its current condition. Photos and repair estimates you already have can help explain the work. You can ask us to review the property without starting a renovation.

We look at the home’s location, condition, nearby sales and the work involved. A direct offer may differ from a price you could achieve through a public listing. Compare both routes before deciding.

What happens after you contact us

Send the address, your connection to the property and your preferred timing. We follow up to understand the home and discuss any access needed. If the property fits our plans, we can discuss an offer.

Review the proposed price, conditions, closing costs and move-out arrangements in writing. An inquiry does not commit you to a sale, and a closing date depends on the property and agreed terms.
