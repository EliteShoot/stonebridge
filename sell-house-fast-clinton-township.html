Tell us how the home is used today

Let us know whether you live at the property, rent it out or have left it vacant. If there is a lease, an association or a move you need to coordinate, include that in your inquiry. These details can affect access, costs and the terms of a sale.

You can begin with a short description. We will follow up about missing details before deciding whether to move forward with a possible purchase.

Consider selling before starting another project

If the house needs updating, compare the work you would have to manage with selling in its current condition. Share known repairs, unfinished projects and any estimates or photos you already have. You do not need to complete a renovation to contact us.

We buy with the planned work and future ownership in mind. When comparing an offer, ask what assumptions it makes about condition and whether any inspections or other conditions remain.

Set expectations for closing and moving

Tell us both when you would like to close and when you could hand over possession. If you are moving to another home or coordinating with tenants, explain what needs to happen first. The final arrangements should be written into the agreement.

Before choosing a buyer, compare the price, costs, conditions and timeline. A StoneBridge inquiry is free of any obligation to sell. We can discuss an offer only after reviewing the property.
