When you submit a property inquiry for a home or land, StoneBridge Development Group may collect information you choose to provide, including your name, phone number, email address, property address or parcel location, selling timeline, and property condition.

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use submitted information to review your home or land inquiry, communicate with you about the property, respond to requests, and evaluate potential acquisition opportunities.

We do not require sensitive financial information to submit a property. Please do not include passwords, Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, or other highly sensitive information in this form.

StoneBridge Development Group does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers or professional partners only when reasonably necessary to operate our business, evaluate a property, communicate with you, or comply with applicable law.

By submitting your information, you are asking StoneBridge Development Group to contact you regarding your inquiry. You are not obligated to accept an offer or complete a transaction.
